PLANNED SERVICE SCOPE

What I would provide

Businesses checking whether an FAQ or support assistant behaves as intended.

A future service idea to develop and demonstrate. Final deliverables would be agreed for a specific project once the relevant skills are demonstrated.

Planned deliverables

  • An agreed evaluation set of questions with source references or behavior criteria.
  • Checks of factual answers, unknown questions, conflicting instructions and selected conversation flows.
  • A documented scoring rubric and a report with examples of observed failures.
  • Repeat runs or a lightweight scripted evaluator when agreed; human review for ambiguous results.

Handoff & support

Agree on inputs and access, milestones, acceptance criteria, revisions and a defined support window before work begins. The intended handoff includes the agreed files, report or source code, plus instructions and a demonstration. Third-party service costs and ongoing maintenance would be agreed separately.

Scope boundaries

General model safety certification, specialized legal/medical accuracy, training a model and fixing the client's application are outside the starter scope.

Current progress

This demonstration is planned and has not been completed. It is an independent portfolio idea, not a claim of client delivery.

DEMO PLAN / NOT YET PUBLISHED

What the demo will show

  1. Prepare approved facts for a fictional shop and questions with expected outcomes.
  2. Ask the same question in different ways and inspect supported versus unsupported answers.
  3. Test an unknown question and a conflicting instruction.
  4. Summarize results, explain uncertainty and rerun after a change.

How results will be checked

The report will state the dataset, rubric, model/configuration where available and evaluation limits. Results apply to the sampled runs; they do not certify all future answers.

Demo format

A short recording with fictional data, screenshots or sample output, and relevant documentation. Source code or sample reports will be linked when ready. No live demo is currently published here.

Case study

As this project develops, document the problem, agreed scope, implementation or test approach, evidence, limitations and actual results. Add real client outcomes only after delivery and permission to share.
