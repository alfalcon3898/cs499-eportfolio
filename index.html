01 / INTRODUCTION

From experience to engineering.

ShiftFlow grew out of firsthand experience managing restaurant teams, where scheduling, availability, and shift coverage can become fragmented across paper and separate systems.

This portfolio documents three capstone enhancement areas: software design and engineering, algorithms and data structures, and databases. Together, they demonstrate how a practical problem can be approached through modular design, testable rules, and persistent data.
