01 / PROFESSIONAL SELF-ASSESSMENT

From experience to engineering.

CS 499 Computer Science Capstone · Southern New Hampshire University

Introduction

My journey in computer science began with a natural interest in problem-solving and curiosity about programming. I have always enjoyed finding solutions to challenges, and coding appealed to me because it combined logical thinking with creativity. The possibility of developing something functional from an idea motivated me to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a concentration in Software Engineering at Southern New Hampshire University.

Throughout my education, I have learned that software development involves much more than writing code. It requires planning, critical thinking, testing, debugging, and continuously improving existing solutions. My experience developing ShiftFlow for my computer science capstone helped me bring these skills together while strengthening my confidence as an aspiring technology professional.

Connecting Professional Experience With Computer Science

My professional background in restaurant management and hospitality has played an important role in shaping how I approach problem-solving. Working in these environments has taught me the importance of organization, communication, time management, and adapting to unexpected challenges.

Through my experience managing restaurant operations, I recognized how employee scheduling could become a complicated process. Employee availability, shift coverage, staffing requirements, and schedule changes all require careful consideration. I saw an opportunity to use programming to address these challenges.

This realization inspired ShiftFlow, an employee scheduling application designed to help organize employee information and manage schedules more efficiently. Rather than developing a project without a practical purpose, I wanted to create something based on a problem I had encountered firsthand.

Developing ShiftFlow allowed me to connect my professional experience with my academic knowledge. It also helped me understand the importance of identifying user needs and designing software around real operational challenges.

Technical Growth and Software Engineering

One of the most important lessons I learned throughout my computer science education was the value of writing organized, maintainable, and testable code.

During the development of ShiftFlow, I faced the challenge of relearning programming concepts while simultaneously applying them to a working application. I needed to revisit concepts, understand how different components interacted, and determine how to structure the program as its functionality expanded.

For my first capstone enhancement, I focused on software design and engineering by improving the application's object-oriented structure. I developed classes representing employees, shifts, and schedules, separating responsibilities and introducing validation to improve the reliability of the application.

This enhancement strengthened my understanding of encapsulation, modularity, and the importance of designing code that can be modified without unnecessarily affecting other components.

My second enhancement focused on algorithms and data structures. I developed scheduling logic to evaluate employee availability, identify overlapping shifts, calculate weekly work hours, and support staffing decisions.

Implementing these features required me to consider different scheduling scenarios and determine how the application should respond to conflicting or invalid information. This process improved my ability to break complex problems into smaller, manageable tasks.

For my third enhancement, I integrated SQLite database functionality to provide persistent storage for employee and scheduling information. This allowed ShiftFlow to retain information between application sessions rather than relying exclusively on temporary in-memory data.

Working with databases strengthened my understanding of data persistence, database operations, and the relationship between application logic and stored information.

Together, these enhancements demonstrate my growth in software design, algorithmic thinking, and database development.

Testing, Debugging, and Problem-Solving

One of the most challenging aspects of developing ShiftFlow was learning how to test the application effectively.

Initially, I found it difficult to determine which scenarios needed testing and how to verify that the program would behave correctly under different conditions. As the application became more complex, I realized that testing was essential to ensuring that new features did not interfere with existing functionality.

I used pytest to develop automated tests and evaluated scenarios involving employee validation, scheduling conflicts, availability restrictions, and database operations. I also performed manual testing through the command-line interface to verify that different features worked together as expected.

These experiences helped me develop a more systematic approach to software development. Instead of assuming that a feature worked because it produced the expected result once, I learned to consider alternative inputs, potential failures, and interactions between components.

I also gained a greater appreciation for debugging. Resolving issues required patience, careful observation, and a willingness to revisit earlier design decisions.

Through this process, I became more comfortable learning independently, researching unfamiliar concepts, and improving my work through repeated testing and refinement.

Collaboration, Communication, and Professional Development

My professional experience has helped me develop skills that extend beyond technical knowledge.

Working in restaurant management and hospitality has required me to communicate with different individuals, coordinate responsibilities, address operational problems, and make decisions in time-sensitive situations.

These experiences have influenced how I approach software development. I understand the importance of considering the people who will use an application, communicating clearly, and designing solutions that address practical needs.

Although much of my capstone development involved independent programming, my academic experience also reinforced the importance of documentation, constructive feedback, and explaining technical decisions.

I recognize that successful software development often requires collaboration among individuals with different responsibilities and areas of expertise. As I enter the technology industry, I intend to continue strengthening both my technical and interpersonal skills.

Career Goals and Future Development

My long-term professional goal is to establish a career in software engineering, where I can continue developing applications that solve real-world problems.

I am also interested in beginning my technology career through quality assurance, test automation, application support, or other entry-level technical positions. I view these opportunities as valuable ways to gain professional experience, develop additional skills, and contribute to software quality and reliability.

Beyond entering the technology industry, I hope to continue developing ShiftFlow into a fully functional application that businesses can use to simplify employee scheduling, manage shift requests, and improve workplace operations.

Future features I would like to explore include AI-assisted scheduling, employee shift-swap requests, and workflows that allow managers to review and approve schedule changes. My long-term ambition is to investigate the possibility of offering ShiftFlow as a commercial product.

I understand that achieving these goals will require continuous learning, practical experience, and further development of my technical abilities.

Conclusion

My computer science education has provided me with a foundation in software engineering, algorithms, databases, and problem-solving. More importantly, it has taught me how to approach unfamiliar challenges, learn independently, and improve software through testing and refinement.

Developing ShiftFlow represents an important milestone in my growth as a programmer. What began as an idea inspired by workplace challenges developed into an application that demonstrates the knowledge and skills I have gained throughout my degree program.

As I move forward, I hope to continue building on this foundation, gaining professional experience, and developing software that provides meaningful solutions to real-world problems.
