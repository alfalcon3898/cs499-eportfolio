PLANNED SERVICE SCOPE

What I would provide

Restaurant teams coordinating employees, shifts and coverage.

This describes a future offer being developed through this project. Deliverables will be confirmed for each engagement once the relevant capability is demonstrated.

Planned deliverables

  • Employee and shift management with availability and conflict checks.
  • Employee pickup requests with manager review and approval.
  • Manager and employee screens with access appropriate to each role.
  • Database persistence, tests of core workflows and setup documentation.

Handoff & support

The intended handoff includes the agreed source code or configuration, setup instructions, a short demonstration and acceptance checks. Before a project starts, we would agree on required access, delivery milestones, revision limits and a defined support window.

Hosting and third-party API accounts, usage charges and ongoing maintenance would be agreed explicitly.

Possible additions

AI-assisted schedule suggestions, swaps, additional notifications and multi-location support would be separately scoped after the core product works.
