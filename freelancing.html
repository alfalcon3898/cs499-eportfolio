02 / HOW I BUILD

Make it work. Understand why.

I work in small steps: understand a problem, implement a feature, test the important cases and document what I learned. As projects mature, each will gain evidence a visitor can inspect:

  • A clear problem and a defined scope.
  • Source code and reproducible setup instructions.
  • A short demo, screenshots or a live example where appropriate.
  • Tests, failure handling and known limitations.
  • A case study describing decisions and verified results.

Independent projects are labeled as such. Client work and testimonials will be added only after actual delivery and permission to share.
