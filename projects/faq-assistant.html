PLANNED SERVICE SCOPE

What I would provide

Businesses across industries answering repeated customer questions, including shops, salons, service companies and restaurants.

This describes a future offer being developed through this project. Deliverables will be confirmed for each engagement once the relevant capability is demonstrated.

Planned deliverables

  • Answers based on a supplied and approved set of business information.
  • A defined topic scope, such as opening hours, services, products, return or cancellation policies and contact details.
  • An unknown-answer response and a contact or handoff option.
  • A simple API or website widget as agreed, with answer evaluations and update instructions.

Handoff & support

The intended handoff includes the agreed source code or configuration, setup instructions, a short demonstration and acceptance checks. Before a project starts, we would agree on required access, delivery milestones, revision limits and a defined support window.

Hosting and third-party API accounts, usage charges and ongoing maintenance would be agreed explicitly.

Possible additions

Booking, ordering, payments, multilingual support and connections to live business systems would require separate scope.

Current progress

No demo has been built yet. The initial scope will use fictional business information, with sample scenarios for a shop, salon or service company. Each business would supply its own approved content.

Planned milestones

  1. Approved information and unknown-question handling
  2. A small API and optional chat interface
  3. Repeatable answer evaluations

DEMO PLAN / NOT YET PUBLISHED

What the demo will show

Explore how an FAQ assistant could be configured for different businesses using their own approved information. Planned examples include a shop's return policy, a salon's services and a service company's operating hours.

Example walkthrough

  1. Ask a fictional shop about a product and return policy, a salon about services and cancellation rules, or a service company about hours and contact details. Compare each answer with that business's approved source.
  2. Ask a question the information does not answer and see the assistant acknowledge the gap and offer the configured contact option.
  3. Change a sample business fact, such as opening hours or a service description, and show how the assistant's information is updated.
  4. Try an instruction that conflicts with the approved information and inspect whether the answer stays within the intended scope.

How the results will be checked

A compact evaluation set will compare expected behavior with actual answers. The demo will distinguish sourced facts from unknown information and document observed limitations.

Demo format

A guided API or chat-widget recording with visible sample source material. A live fictional-data demo may be added after implementation.
